# Third-party and original content provenance

Acquisition date: 2026-10-20

## Selection policy

The Bible text in this repository was acquired directly from [eBible.org](https://ebible.org/), not from the analyzed APK or an unattributed mirror. Each selected package is marked redistributable in eBible's `translations.csv`, contains the complete 39-book Old Testament and 27-book New Testament, and has a translation-specific rights notice. eBible's [intellectual-property policy](https://ebible.org/ippolicy.php) says, “You may copy any Public Domain work freely, and in any format you please,” while requiring users of Creative Commons works to follow the exact attached license.

Direct archive URLs are mutable. The source SHA-256 values below identify the exact bytes acquired and normalized on the acquisition date. Source archives are not redistributed in this repository.

That means that it is not copyrighted.” The notice identifies the 2020 stable text edition. “World English Bible” is an eBible.org trademark; format normalization did not alter the Scripture wording, and a textually modified derivative must use a different name.

### Portuguese license-version risk

The exact eBible archive and its attached notice say CC BY 4.0, so that is the license recorded in `translations.json`. However, the rights holders' [Bíblia Livre repository README](https://github.com/blivre/BibliaLivre) says “Licença Creative Commons Atribuição 3.0 Brasil.” Both notices permit redistribution and adaptation with attribution, but the version discrepancy is unresolved. Redistribution should conservatively preserve the full credit above, link both the [CC BY 4.0 deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and [CC BY 3.0 Brazil deed](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/br/), identify format changes, and avoid implying endorsement. Obtain clarification from the credited authors if a single unambiguous license identifier is a release requirement. This is a 2018 Almeida-derived modernization, not a verbatim public-domain 1819 or 1911 Almeida edition.

### Remaining provenance and quality caveats

- eBible's catalogue marks `porbr2018` and `russyn` as `Certified=False`. Their package identity, complete book inventory, downloadability, and rights notices are explicit, but this work does not independently establish ecclesiastical or publisher certification of those electronic transcriptions.
- Public-domain status is recorded from each edition's attached eBible notice and is not an independent legal opinion for every jurisdiction. The historical dates and explicit package notices support redistribution, but a distributor with jurisdiction-specific requirements should perform its own review.
- None of these packages declares a shared formal versification mapping. Chapter coverage is the same canonical 1,189 chapters, while verse numbering and populated verse totals legitimately differ by edition.

### Normalization

- Selected exactly the canonical Protestant IDs `GEN` through `REV` in their standard 66-book order; front matter, glossaries, and any noncanonical material were excluded.
- Took localized book names from each USFM file's `toc2` field, falling back to `h` only if needed.
- Preserved chapter and verse numbering and Scripture wording while removing USFM control markup, Strong's attributes, footnotes, and cross-references. Supplied words marked `add` remain visibly bracketed. Psalm descriptive headings and in-verse Song of Songs speaker labels follow eBible's own verse-per-line rendering.
- Normalized Unicode to NFC and collapsed formatting whitespace. Every populated USFM verse was compared byte-for-text with the corresponding eBible `*_vpl.zip` rendering before the compact JSON was emitted; all seven comparisons had zero differences.
- Omitted source verse markers whose main text was empty after notes were removed. Therefore `verseCount` means nonempty stored entries and deliberately does not assume the 31,102 count associated with one versification.

`assets/bible/translations.json` is the authoritative compact manifest. `tool/verify_content.mjs` verifies its exact schema, assets, hashes, counts, canonical book order, per-book chapter completeness, verse structure, and the provenance values on this page.
