# Holy Calendar scope

The Holy Calendar is an offline, original landmark calendar. It generates the
principal fixed and movable celebrations for the selected tradition and
locale. It intentionally does not claim to be a complete daily lectionary and
does not reproduce copyrighted liturgical readings.

Movable western dates use the Gregorian computus. Orthodox Paschal dates use
the Julian computus converted to the civil calendar for modern years. Fixed
Orthodox dates follow the civil dates used by the Orthodox Church in America's
2026 calendar; local jurisdictions that retain the Old Calendar can differ.

Reference checks for 2026:

- USCCB, *Liturgical Calendar for the Dioceses of the United States of America
  2026*: Easter on April 5 and First Sunday of Advent on November 29.
  <https://www.usccb.org/resources/2026cal.pdf>
- Orthodox Church in America, *Lenten and Paschal Cycle*: Pascha on April 12,
  Ascension on May 21 and Pentecost on May 31.
  <https://www.oca.org/fs/paschal-cycle>

The generated data is cached by tradition, locale, and civil year. A refresh
rebuilds the current year's calendar without requiring a network request.
